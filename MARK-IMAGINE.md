# MARK + IMAGINE brief — Panel Bay

## Brand mark
- Site / brand name: Panel Bay
- What the name means: a bay is one fence panel’s width, usually 1.83 m in the UK
- Mood words: measured, outdoor, blunt, daylight, timber
- Must not look like: aivora.digital, a soft Amazon guide, a timber-merchant logo, a map pin
- Mark direction: geometric SVG. A simple bay (two posts and one panel gap) or a tape-tick. Sharp at 16×16. Not a soft AI blob. Not a photo of a fence as the logo.

### Process
1. This brief.
2. Optional: 3–5 Imagine explorations for mark ideas only.
3. Ship a clean geometric SVG as the live header.
4. Favicon + OG pack from that mark.

## Imagery
- Prefer Grok Imagine on grok.com for the one hero.
- No scene captions on the image.
- Bright or neutral daylight. No default rainy UK gloom.
- A real close-board or panel fence, posts in the ground, not a fence drawn on the outside of a house by mistake.

### Shot list
| Page / job | Shot needed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Home hero | One daylight run of timber fence panels, shot along the line so the bays read | No text on the image. Not a product packshot. |
| Calculator result | No photo. The numbers are the visual. | |

## Anti-spam
Do not wallpaper sections with AI filler. One hero plus the mark.
