Height

Panel Bay counts materials. It does not say how high a fence can be.

The rules are written on the Planning Portal. Your council applies them to the address. Read both before you order a tall panel. A fence beside a road, a listed building, and a fence that replaces a higher one are the cases that catch people out.

  • Fences, gates and garden walls on the Planning Portal
  • Find your council on the Planning Portal
