Postcrete bags

The bags follow the post and the hole.

The holes this tally uses

A 100 × 100 mm post, in a hole 300 mm across and 600 mm deep, takes 2 bags of 20 kg. That is the Heidelberg PostFix figure for June 2025.

A 75 × 75 mm post, in a hole 250 mm across and 600 mm deep, is counted at 1½ bags. That is a rough allowance, not a manufacturer row. The tally multiplies it out and rounds up, because bags are sold whole.

A wider hole, soft ground, or a heavy gate needs more than this. Read the bag before you load the car.

Panel Bay counts one run. It does not sell timber, and it does not give planning advice.
