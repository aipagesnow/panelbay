Measure the run in metres. The tally is the panels, posts, Postcrete and gravel boards for that one line.

How this tally counts

A bay is one panel width. The box starts at 1.83 m, the usual UK panel. Change it if yours are a different width.

Full bays are whole panels. If the last bay comes up short, the tally calls it a cut panel. You still buy a whole panel and saw it down. A remainder of 20 mm or less is taken up in the joints, and it is not a cut. If the tape is up to 20 mm short of another full panel, that panel is counted and there is nothing to cut. Either way, the tally shows the millimetres.

A gate is taken out of the run before the panels are counted. It still counts as a bay for the posts. It does not get a gravel board.

Posts are one more than the bays, unless an end fixes to a wall. Postcrete follows the post size and the hole on the Postcrete page, in 20 kg bags.

Panel Bay counts one run. It does not sell timber, and it does not give planning advice.
