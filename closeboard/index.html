Board width

How it counts

The run is split into equal bays no wider than your post spacing, and each bay gets a post at each end, so posts are one more than bays. The posts page has lengths and depths. Boards are counted along the whole run at the cover width and rounded up. Rails depend on the height. Postcrete is counted at 2 bags of 20 kg for each 100 mm post in a 300 × 600 mm hole, from the Blue Circle table.

Questions

How many feather edge boards per metre?

With 125 mm boards overlapped by 25 mm, each board covers 100 mm, so 10 boards a metre. With 100 mm boards the cover is 75 mm, so 13⅓ a metre. Buy a few spare for splits and offcuts.

How far apart should closeboard posts be?

No more than 3 m between centres. Closer posts make a stiffer fence, which helps in exposed gardens.

How many arris rails do I need?

Two rails for a fence up to about 1.2 m, three up to about 1.95 m, and four above that. Each bay gets its own set of rails.

How do I nail feather edge boards?

One 50 mm galvanised nail per board at each rail, about 20 mm in from the thick edge. Do not nail through both boards where they overlap, so each board can move with the weather.

What length of post for a closeboard fence?

A 2.4 m post for a fence 1.8 m high, with 600 mm in the ground. Mortised or V-notched posts take the arris rails.

Is closeboard stronger than panels?

Usually, yes. The boards are fixed one by one to rails set into the posts, so there is no lightweight panel frame to fail, and a damaged board can be swapped on its own. It takes longer to build.
