# STYLE — Panel Bay

Use only for this Grok Project (panelbay.co.uk). Do not import Aivora Digital, Lock Desk, Amazon review sites, freeuktools, IfSketch, or the Garden map app.

## Mood
- measured
- outdoor
- blunt
- daylight
- timber

## Visual archetype
A surveyor’s tally on a close-board fence: dark stained timber, sharp white type, one high-vis tape-yellow accent. Numbers are the hero, not a photo card.

## Anti-references
- aivora.digital (cream, soft teal/sage, rounded package cards, copper hire chrome)
- Soft Amazon buying-guide pages
- Merchant calculators that look like a Wickes or B&Q tool (busy promo bars, product grids)
- The Garden satellite-map app

## Type
- Display: Barlow Condensed
- Body: Source Sans 3
- Unique pair. Not a serif editorial look.

## Colour
- Background: near-black stained timber, not cream
- Text: off-white
- Accent: high-vis tape yellow, used sparingly on the result numbers
- Avoid cream + soft teal/sage + pillowy cards
- No Aivora copper #C45C2A

## Hero / first look
- Mobile: the calculator and its title are both visible without a frosted card over a washed photo
- The result (panel count, posts, bags) is the first thing after the inputs
- One real fence photo at most, not a gallery

## Hard must-nots
- No map or satellite measure tool
- No novel / book prose
- No leftover notes, TODOs, or insider build jargon on public pages
- No Amazon “best of” layout
