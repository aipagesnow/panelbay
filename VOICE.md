# VOICE — Panel Bay

Writing rules for panelbay.co.uk only. Fresh voice. Not Amazon clear-reviewer, not Aivora hire, not a shop assistant.

## Who is speaking
A person who has counted fence bays before. Plain, specific, a bit short. Not a brand.

## Who is reading
Someone in the UK with a tape measure, about to buy panels, posts, and Postcrete for one run of fence.

## Aim for
- Say the number, then the reason
- Use the words they already use: bay, panel, post, gravel board, Postcrete
- UK English

## Avoid
- Magazine cleverness, hype, taglines
- Aivora hire voice
- Review-site “we compared” voice
- Fake first-hand testing
- Legal advice. Height notes point at the council or Planning Portal. Do not say what is allowed.

## Sentence habits
- Complete sentences. Mix of lengths.
- Clarity over cleverness
- If a line could sit on any calculator site, rewrite it

## Pages this voice covers
- Home calculator
- Posts for a run
- Postcrete bags
- Gravel boards
- Height note (pointer only, not advice)
